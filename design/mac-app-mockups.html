Background work leaves a complete record

Schedules state their skip conditions up front. History distinguishes completed, skipped, failed, cancelled, and missed work; “finished” is never used for work that did not run.

SCHEDULES · HISTORYLaunch at login · one task at a time

Tasks run whenever the Mac and Syncy are awake, including on battery. Unavailable destinations are recorded as skipped.
